# Inventory Module Documentation

## Inventory Management Overview
- **Description**: The inventory module tracks stock levels and manages warehouse operations.
- **Key Features**:
  - Stock Tracking: Monitor inventory levels in real-time.
  - Warehouse Management: Organize and manage warehouse locations.
  - Inventory Reports: Generate reports on stock levels and movements.

## Workflows
- **Stock Tracking Workflow**:
  1. Receive goods into inventory.
  2. Update stock levels.
  3. Track stock movements.

- **Warehouse Management Workflow**:
  1. Assign storage locations.
  2. Organize inventory by categories.
  3. Conduct regular audits.

- **Inventory Reporting Workflow**:
  1. Generate stock level reports.
  2. Analyze stock movements.
  3. Identify slow-moving items.
